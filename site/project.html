Final project

40% of the course grade · teams of four · Weeks 9–15

Your team works as a research team for a client. The client has an AI system, some data, and a decision to make. Your team designs and carries out a statistical study to inform that decision, then presents it to the client as one clear argument in slides and a video of up to eight minutes.

Project documents

Two documents hold every project requirement. Slides, Canvas pages, and notebooks point back to them. If anything else disagrees with these two documents, they win.
