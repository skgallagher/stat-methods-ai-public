Final project

40% of the course grade · teams of four · Weeks 9–15

Your team works as a research team for a client. The client has an AI system, some data, and a decision to make. Your team designs and carries out a statistical study to inform that decision, then presents it to the client as one clear argument in slides and a five-minute video.

Project documents

Two documents hold every project requirement. Slides, Canvas pages, and notebooks point back to them. If anything else disagrees with these two documents, they win.
