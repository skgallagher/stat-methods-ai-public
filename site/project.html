Final project

40% of the course grade · teams of four · Weeks 9–15

You act as statistical consultants. A client has an AI system, some data, and a decision to make, and your team designs and carries out a statistical study to inform that decision.

What every project includes

  • a simple reference point, such as always predicting the most common class;
  • a statistical baseline your team fits and can explain, usually logistic regression;
  • uncertainty for each key estimate;
  • a fair comparison between the baseline and the AI system;
  • one sensitivity analysis that could change the conclusion;
  • a recommendation that matches the evidence.

Four tracks

TrackClient questionData and AI system

Data, cached AI outputs, and starter code are provided for every track. Teams and track choices are set by the midterm.
