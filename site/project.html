What every team does

Required analysis

  1. 1
    Frame the studyTarget population, unit, estimand, comparison, and decision.
  2. 2
    Explore the benchmarkMeasurement, labels, dependence, hard cases, and mismatch.
  3. 3
    Own a baselineBuild and explain a simple, defensible comparison model.
  1. 4
    Evaluate the AI systemUse paired cases plus uncertainty or calibration evidence.
  2. 5
    Test sensitivityVary one important assumption, threshold, prompt, or labeling choice.
  3. 6
    Recommend—carefullyState limitations, guardrails, and what would change the conclusion.

Deliverables

Checkpoint 1

Week 11
