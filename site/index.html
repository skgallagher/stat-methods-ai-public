About the course

We study AI systems the way a statistician would. Who do the numbers describe? How were the data and labels made? How uncertain are the results, and is a comparison fair? Along the way, you will use AI tools in your own statistical work and check what they give you.

This is not a survey of machine-learning algorithms. We learn about AI systems only as far as we need to evaluate them.

A typical week

Tuesday introduces a statistical problem and works through examples. Thursday pairs a shorter lecture with a hands-on lab in Google Colab, and the homework picks up where the lab leaves off. A short checkpoint quiz on Canvas closes out the week. After spring break, the course turns to the team project.
