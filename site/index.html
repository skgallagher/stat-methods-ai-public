The recurring workflow

Five moves, every week

The method changes. The statistical habits do not.

  1. 1 Research a hypothesis Population, unit, estimand, comparison, decision.
  2. 2 Explore the evidence Measurement, dependence, hard cases, mismatch.
  3. 3 Model and compare Own a baseline. Compare fairly. Quantify uncertainty.
  4. 4 Test sensitivity Change one consequential assumption on purpose.
  5. 5 Decide—carefully Recommend within the limits of the evidence.

A recurring translation

AI language, statistical questions

benchmark→sampling design
accuracy→estimate + uncertainty
confidence→calibration question
ablation→sensitivity analysis
human feedback→measurement protocol

Course information

The essentials

Format

  • Tuesday/Thursday meetings
  • Lecture plus guided lab or project studio
  • Python in Google Colab; CPU-only by default

Assessment

  • Seven homeworks; lowest score dropped
  • Closed-book, no-AI midterm in Week 7
  • Team project with checkpoints and oral discussion

AI use

  • AI tools are part of the course
  • Prompts, outputs, and revisions are documented
  • Generation may be delegated; judgment may not
