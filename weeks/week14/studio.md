Today you get your storyboard back on paper, with comments, and your team meets with the instructor for about five minutes. Bring your slides as they stand, and bring questions.

## Session plan

| Time | Do |

## While you wait for your meeting

1. **Read the comments** on your storyboard, and fix your slide titles first.
2. **Title test.** Read only your slide titles, in order, to a neighboring team. The listeners say what your recommendation is and which title lost them.
3. **Timed run.** Give that team your full eight minutes, with a timer. They name **one cut**. Then swap.
4. **Cards.** Each member draws a card below and explains it for 90 seconds, without notes.
5. **Exit ticket.** Before you leave, each member fills one in on their own.

## The cards

**Baseline** (what it uses and ignores, why it is a fair comparison) · **main comparison** (the paired difference and its interval) · **calibration** (what it showed and what it means for the decision) · **sensitivity analysis** (the pre-stated rule and what happened) · **AI use** (one thing you checked or changed). The member who cannot explain a card is the team's risk in the oral presentation. Fix it this weekend.

## Individual exit ticket

Each member, separately: "The part of our project I would be weakest defending is ___." The instructor reads these before writing oral presentation questions. An honest answer earns a fair question, not a gotcha.
